# Statutory Compliance & Legal Disclaimer

**IMPORTANT NOTICE TO ALL USERS AND ADMINISTRATORS:**

1. **Educational & Planning Purpose Only**  
   This application is developed strictly for educational, planning, and preliminary computation purposes. It does not constitute formal tax, legal, or accounting advice.

2. **Statutory Verification Required**  
   Tax calculations and legal comparisons generated by this workstation must be independently verified against the latest relevant provisions of the **Income-tax Act, 1961**, the configured drafts/provisions of the **Income-tax Act, 2025**, the annual Finance Acts, Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) notifications, circulars, and certified tax professionals.

3. **Rule Configuration Principle**  
   The application intentionally contains no hard-coded statutory tax rates or unverified legal assumptions. All slab brackets, rates, deductions, exemptions, and thresholds are loaded dynamically from the local database. If a statutory rule has not been configured by the user or administrator, the system displays **"Rule not configured"** instead of making assumptions.

4. **No Government Affiliation**  
   This software is an independent offline utility and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Income Tax Department, the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT), the Ministry of Finance, or the Government of India.
